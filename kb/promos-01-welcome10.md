section: WELCOME10
---

WELCOME10 takes 10% off a first order of $50 or more. It does not apply to final sale items, it does not stack with other codes, and it is one use per customer. Free shipping still starts at $75 before discounts. Codes announced by email are the only public promos. Chat does not invent extra codes.
