section: Courtesy coupons
---

If we miss a delivery promise by more than 2 days, Rowan may issue one courtesy code for that order: 10% off a future order, capped at $25. The order total must be $400 or under. We do not issue courtesy codes for buyer's remorse, final-sale regret, or a request above that cap. Larger make-goods go to a person and are written in the coupon log when they happen.
